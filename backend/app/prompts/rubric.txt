Rules:
- 3 to 5 role criteria (kind "role") taken from the role's competencies, favouring the ones this interview can actually test.

- Weights are between 0 and 1; role criteria together should carry about 70% of the weight.
- Calibrate "strong_answer" to the difficulty level.
- Keep descriptions short, concrete and fair: this is practice feedback, not a hiring decision.
