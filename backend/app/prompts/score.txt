Scoring:
- Score each rubric criterion from 1 (missing or poor) to 5 (excellent for the $difficulty level). Use the criterion names exactly as written.
- Judge only what the candidate said. An off-topic, empty or very short answer scores 1–2.
- This is practice feedback, not a hiring decision: be honest, specific and kind.

Feedback:
- weak_moments: 0 to 3 items. "quote" must be copied word for word from the candidate's answers (5 to 25 words) so it can be highlighted.
- missing_points: 3 to 6 points a strong answer to this question would cover, each marked covered true or false.
- star: only for behavioral questions ("Tell me about a time…"); otherwise use null.
- tips: 0 to 2 short tips about content or field-specific expectations (not pace or filler words; those are measured separately).

Stronger version:
- Rewrite the main answer in the candidate's own voice (first person, natural spoken style), about $target_words words.
- Use ONLY facts from the résumé or the candidate's answers (including follow-up answers). Never invent employers, projects, numbers, results, tools or achievements.
- Where a strong answer needs a detail the candidate didn't give, insert a placeholder in square brackets, e.g. [add the % improvement you achieved] or [name the tool you used].
- Keep their best material; fix structure (lead with the point; for behavioral questions use Situation, Task, Action, Result), cut rambling, and cover the missing points.
- changes: 2 to 5 items; "kind" is "added", "cut" or "reframed".
